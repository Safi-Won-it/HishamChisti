GOOD QUESTIONS

Buyers & sellers ask me…

How much do I need for a down payment?

In Canada, as little as 5% on the first $500,000 for an insured mortgage (with conditions), more above that. I’ll walk you through the exact math for your price range — no obligation.

What does it cost to use you as a buyer?

In most resale purchases the buyer’s brokerage is paid from the seller’s commission arrangement — I’ll explain your exact agreement in writing before we start, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with first-time buyers and newcomers?

Yes — it’s one of my favourite parts of the job. I explain every step, help with pre-approval and incentives, and never rush you.

Can you help with commercial leases and business purchases?

Yes. From Danforth storefronts to plazas across the GTA, I handle commercial leasing and business sales — including competitive, multi-offer situations.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto (Scarborough, North York, Danforth / Bangla Town), Pickering & Durham, Brampton, Markham and the wider GTA.
